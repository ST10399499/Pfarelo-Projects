      About us

      Our Mission

      At Pfarelo Projects, we are on a mission to make our communities a cleaner, greener, and healthier place for all. Our primary focus is on trash picking and environmental conservation efforts to combat pollution and protect the natural world we all share.


      Our Story

      Pfarelo Projects was founded in 2019 by a group of passionate environmentalists who were deeply concerned about the growing issue of trash pollution in their local community. What began as a small group of dedicated volunteers picking up litter in their neighborhood has now evolved into a thriving NGO with a global impact.


      Over the years, Pfarelo Projects has organized countless clean-up drives, educational programs, and advocacy campaigns to raise awareness about the importance of responsible waste disposal and recycling. Our journey has been marked by challenges, victories, and unwavering dedication to our cause.

      Our Team

      Behind Pfarelo Projects's success is a diverse and committed team of individuals who share a common love for the environment and a determination to drive positive change. Our team comprises experts in environmental science, community outreach, project management, and communications.


      Meet some of the key members of our team:

      Pfarelo Luvhimbi - Founder and Executive Director: Pfarelo's vision and leadership have been instrumental in guiding Pfarelo Projects's growth and impact. With a background in environmental science, he is a tireless advocate for sustainable practices.


      Robert Gumede - Community Engagement Specialist: Robert works closely with local communities to foster collaboration and empower individuals to take an active role in cleaning and conserving their surroundings.


      Siyabonga Khumalo - Project Coordinator: Siyabonga oversees the planning and execution of our cleaning initiatives, ensuring they are efficient, safe, and environmentally responsible.


      Ayakha Gwegwana - Communications Manager: Ayakha is responsible for spreading the word about Pfarelo Projects's mission and achievements. He believes that effective communication is key to inspiring change.
